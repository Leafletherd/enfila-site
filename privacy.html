Privacy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

Enfila runs entirely on your Mac. It does not collect, transmit, or store your data anywhere else.

What we collect

Nothing. Enfila has no analytics, no tracking, and no accounts. The app does not phone home.

Your files

Enfila reads the music you stage and writes it to the destination you choose. Files stay on your machine and the device you connect. Conversions happen locally in a temporary folder and never leave your Mac.

License activation

To activate a license, Enfila sends only your license key to our payment provider (Lemon Squeezy) to verify it. No other information is transmitted.

The website

This site uses no analytics, no cookies, and no tracking scripts.

Hosting

Our website is hosted by Netlify. As our hosting provider, Netlify may process basic technical data (such as server logs and IP addresses) and handles submissions sent through the forms on this site. Their handling of that data is governed by Netlify's privacy policy: netlify.com/privacy.

Purchases

If you buy Enfila, checkout is handled by a third-party payment processor. We receive only what's needed to deliver your license. We never see your full card details.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email Hello@enfila.app.
